Typing Speed Test

Choose a test length, click the box and type the text shown. The timer starts with your first key. Your speed is measured in words per minute (WPM), where one "word" is 5 characters: type 200 correct characters in one minute and you score 40 WPM. The average adult types about 35–40 WPM.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

How WPM is calculated

WPM = correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes Raw WPM = all characters typed ÷ 5 ÷ minutes Accuracy = (keys pressed − mistakes) ÷ keys pressed × 100 Example: 230 correct characters in 1 minute → 230 ÷ 5 = 46 WPM

Typing tests count every 5 characters (including spaces) as one word, so long and short words are measured fairly. Only correct characters count towards your WPM, so typing fast with lots of mistakes doesn't help. Accuracy counts every wrong key you pressed, even if you fixed it with backspace.

What's a good typing speed?

WPMLevel
Under 25Beginner, often still looking at the keys
25–40Average; most adults are around 35–40
40–60Good; comfortable for most office jobs
60–80Fast; typical of professional typists and programmers
80+Very fast; competitive typists reach 120 and more

Jobs that list a typing requirement often ask for 40–60 WPM with at least 90–95% accuracy. Practise with the same test length the employer uses, as most people are a little faster over 1 minute than over 5.

How to type faster

  1. Use the home row. Rest your left fingers on A S D F and right fingers on J K L ;. The bumps on F and J let you find them without looking.
  2. Use all ten fingers. Each finger is responsible for its own column of keys. Two-finger typists rarely get past 40 WPM.
  3. Don't look at the keyboard. It feels slower at first, but it's the only way to get fast.
  4. Accuracy first. Slow down until you make almost no mistakes. Correcting errors costs more time than typing carefully.
  5. Practise little and often: 10–15 minutes a day beats one long session a week.
  6. Sit well: wrists level, elbows at about 90 degrees, screen at eye height.

Choosing the text

  • Easy uses the most common English words in a random order, which is good for building finger habits.
  • Paragraphs are ordinary sentences with capitals and punctuation, closest to everyday typing.
  • Hard adds numbers, symbols and prices, like typing invoices or code. Expect a lower score.

Pasting is turned off so the result is fair. On a phone, the test measures your touchscreen typing speed, which is usually 30–40 WPM for experienced users.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good WPM?

Around 40 WPM is average for adults. 60 WPM and above is fast, and 80 WPM or more is very fast.

How is typing speed calculated?

Every 5 correct characters, including spaces, count as one word. WPM is that number of words divided by the minutes taken.

What is the difference between WPM and raw WPM?

WPM counts only correct characters. Raw WPM counts everything you typed, including mistakes.

How can I improve my typing speed?

Learn touch typing on the home row, use all ten fingers, stop looking at the keys, and practise for 10–15 minutes every day, focusing on accuracy first.

Does the typing test work on mobile?

Yes. It measures your typing speed on a touchscreen keyboard, which is usually slower than a physical keyboard.
