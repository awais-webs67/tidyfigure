Birthday Card Maker

Pick a design, type the birthday person's name and your message, add their age or a photo if you like, and download the card as an image. It's sized for WhatsApp and Instagram (square), stories (9:16) or printing at 5 × 7 inches. No sign-up, no watermark.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

How to make your card

  1. Choose a design: Confetti, Balloons, Gold & navy, Minimal or Kids.
  2. Personalise it: their name, age, your message and who it's from. Add a photo to place it in a circle at the top.
  3. Pick the size for where you'll send it.
  4. Download and send it in a chat, post it, or print it.

Birthday message ideas

  • For a friend: "Here's to another year of terrible jokes and great memories. Happy birthday!"
  • For family: "Thank you for always being there. Wishing you the happiest birthday and the best year yet."
  • For a partner: "Every year with you is my favourite. Happy birthday, my love."
  • For a colleague: "Happy birthday! Wishing you a fantastic day and a great year ahead."
  • For a milestone: "Thirty looks good on you! Here's to the best decade yet."
  • For a child: "Happy birthday, superstar! Have the most amazing day."

Sizes explained

  • Square (1080 × 1080): best for WhatsApp, Instagram posts and Facebook.
  • Story (1080 × 1920): fills the screen in Instagram, WhatsApp and Facebook stories.
  • Printable 5 × 7 inches (1500 × 2100): prints sharply at 300 DPI on standard photo paper.

Printing tips

For a physical card, download the printable size and print it on glossy or matte photo paper at "actual size". Most high-street and online photo services accept the PNG directly.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a birthday card with a name online?

Pick a design, type the name and your message, and download the card as an image.

Can I add a photo to the birthday card?

Yes. Upload a photo and it appears in a circle at the top of the card.

What size is best for WhatsApp?

Square (1080 × 1080) works best in chats; use Story size for WhatsApp Status.

Can I print the card?

Yes. Choose Printable 5 × 7 in, which prints sharply on standard photo paper.

Is there a watermark?

No. Cards download without any watermark.
