Sign PDF

To sign a PDF, draw your signature with your mouse or finger (or type your name in a handwriting style), open the PDF, and tap where the signature should go. You can add today's date, place signatures or initials on several pages, and download the signed copy. Nothing is uploaded.

How to sign a PDF

  1. Create your signature. Draw it in the box (a phone or tablet with a finger or stylus gives the most natural result) or type your name and pick a handwriting style.
  2. Open the PDF. The first page appears on the right.
  3. Tap to place. Tap the signature line. Use the arrows to move between pages and the slider to change the size.
  4. Download. The signature is added as an image on the page, exactly where you placed it.

Is an electronic signature legally valid?

In most countries, yes, for most everyday documents. Laws such as the US ESIGN Act and UETA, the EU's eIDAS regulation, and the UK's Electronic Communications Act recognise electronic signatures. A drawn or typed signature on a PDF is a "simple electronic signature": it shows intent to sign, which is enough for things like rental forms, permission slips, quotes, invoices and many contracts.

Some documents need more: wills, property deeds, certain court and government forms, and documents that must be witnessed or notarised. Others require an "advanced" or "qualified" digital signature linked to a verified identity. If in doubt, check with the person or organisation asking for your signature.

Tips for a good-looking signature

  • Draw it larger than you need; the tool crops it tightly and you can shrink it with the slider.
  • Blue ink makes it obvious the document was signed, rather than printed with a signature already on it.
  • Use the date option to add the signing date underneath.
  • For initials on every page, type your initials, then tap each page in turn.

Privacy

Your signature and document never leave your browser. Nothing is stored after you close the page, so nobody can reuse your signature later. That's safer than services that keep a copy of your signature on their servers.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign a PDF for free?

Draw or type your signature, open the PDF, tap where it goes and download. There is no account, watermark or limit.

Can I sign a PDF on my phone?

Yes. Draw your signature with your finger, open the PDF from your phone’s files and tap where to place it.

Is a signature added this way legally binding?

Simple electronic signatures are accepted for most everyday documents in most countries. Some documents, like wills and deeds, need witnessed or certified signatures.

Can I add initials to every page?

Yes. Type your initials, then go through the pages and tap where each one goes.

Is my signature stored anywhere?

No. Your signature and PDF stay in your browser and are gone when you close the page.
