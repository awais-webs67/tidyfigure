Body Fat Calculator

You can estimate body fat with just a tape measure. The US Navy method uses your height and the circumference of your neck and waist (plus hips for women), and is usually within about 3–4 percentage points of lab tests. Healthy ranges are roughly 14–24% for men and 21–31% for women.

The US Navy formula

Men: %BF = 495 ÷ (1.0324 − 0.19077 × log10(waist − neck) + 0.15456 × log10(height)) − 450 Women: %BF = 495 ÷ (1.29579 − 0.35004 × log10(waist + hip − neck) + 0.22100 × log10(height)) − 450 (all measurements in centimetres)

The US military developed it to assess fitness quickly without lab equipment. It's based on the fact that fat collects around the waist and hips, while neck size reflects overall frame and muscle.

How to measure

  • Use a flexible tape, snug but not compressing the skin, and measure in the morning before eating.
  • Neck: just below the Adam's apple (larynx), tape sloping slightly down at the front.
  • Waist: men at the navel; women at the narrowest point. Stand relaxed and breathe out normally.
  • Hips (women): at the widest point of the buttocks.
  • Take each measurement twice and use the average.

Body fat versus BMI

BMI only uses height and weight, so it can label a muscular person "overweight". Body fat percentage measures what you actually care about. Tracking your waist measurement and body fat every few weeks shows progress even when the scale doesn't move, for example when you gain muscle while losing fat.

Other measurement methods

DEXA scans are the most accurate widely available method. Bioimpedance scales are convenient but vary with hydration. Skinfold calipers are accurate in trained hands. For tracking change over time, consistency matters more than the method.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure my body fat at home?

Measure your height, neck and waist (and hips for women) with a tape measure and enter them here. The US Navy method estimates your body fat percentage.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

Roughly 14–24% for men and 21–31% for women, according to the American Council on Exercise.

How accurate is the US Navy body fat formula?

Usually within about 3–4 percentage points of laboratory methods, if measurements are taken carefully.

Is body fat percentage better than BMI?

For individuals, usually yes, because it distinguishes fat from muscle.

Can I use inches?

Yes. Switch to inches and enter every measurement in inches.
