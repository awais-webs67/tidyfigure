How to install it

  1. Unzip the pack and upload all the files to the root folder of your website (where your homepage is).
  2. Paste the HTML snippet into the <head> of every page (in WordPress, use your theme's Site Icon setting or a header plugin instead).
  3. Reload your site. Browsers cache favicons aggressively, so you may need a hard refresh or a private window to see the new one.

Designing a favicon that works at 16 pixels

  • Keep it simple. One or two letters, or a single bold symbol. Detailed logos turn into a blur at 16 × 16.
  • Use strong contrast. It must be visible on light and dark browser tabs.
  • Fill the space. Small icons with lots of empty space look tiny next to other tabs.
  • Check the small previews. The 16 px and 32 px previews are shown enlarged so you can see every pixel.

Why favicons matter for SEO

Google shows your favicon next to your site name in mobile and desktop search results. A clear, recognisable icon makes your result stand out and can improve click-through. Google requires the favicon to be a multiple of 48 px square, which this pack's favicon.ico includes.

Private by design

Icons are drawn in your browser and zipped on your device. Your logo is never uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a favicon.ico?

Type a letter or upload your logo, choose colours and shape, and download the pack. favicon.ico is included with 16, 32 and 48 px sizes inside.

What size should a favicon be?

Provide several: 16 and 32 px for tabs, 48 px for Google, 180 px for Apple devices, and 192 and 512 px for Android.

Where do I put favicon files?

Upload them to the root folder of your website and add the HTML snippet to the of your pages.

Why isn’t my new favicon showing?

Browsers cache favicons. Try a hard refresh, a private window, or wait a day.

Can I use an emoji as a favicon?

Yes. Type the emoji in the text box. Emoji look slightly different on each operating system.
