Cron Expression Generator

A cron expression has five fields: minute hour day-of-month month day-of-week. 30 9 * * 1-5 means "at 9:30 am, Monday to Friday", and */5 * * * * means every 5 minutes. Type or build an expression below to see it in plain English and the next times it will run.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Cron syntax

┌───────────── minute (0–59) │ ┌─────────── hour (0–23) │ │ ┌───────── day of month (1–31) │ │ │ ┌─────── month (1–12 or JAN–DEC) │ │ │ │ ┌───── day of week (0–6, Sunday = 0 or 7, or SUN–SAT) │ │ │ │ │ 30 9 * * 1-5 → 09:30 every weekday

The day-of-month and day-of-week rule

When both day fields are restricted, standard cron runs when either one matches, not both. 0 0 13 * 5 runs on every 13th and every Friday, not only on Friday the 13th. The next-run list above follows this rule, so you can check what will really happen.

Where cron expressions are used

  • Linux crontab: edit with crontab -e, then a line like 30 9 * * 1-5 /home/me/backup.sh.
  • GitHub Actions: on: schedule: - cron: '30 9 * * 1-5'. Always UTC, and runs can be delayed at busy times.
  • Kubernetes CronJobs, cPanel cron jobs, cloud schedulers and CI tools.

Some systems add extras: Quartz and Spring use a 6th field for seconds and support L (last), W (weekday) and # (nth weekday). The checker here follows standard 5-field cron and tells you if it sees those extensions.

Time zones

Cron runs in the server's time zone, which is very often UTC. A job set for 0 9 * * * on a UTC server runs at 9:00 UTC: that's 10:00 in London in winter, 4 or 5 am in New York, and 2:30 pm in India. The next runs above are shown in your own time zone, so if your server uses UTC, adjust the hour.

Frequently asked questions

How do I run a cron job every 5 minutes?

Use */5 * * * *. The */5 in the minute field means every fifth minute.

What does * * * * * mean in cron?

Run every minute of every hour of every day.

What time zone does cron use?

The server’s time zone, often UTC. GitHub Actions schedules always use UTC.

Is Sunday 0 or 7 in cron?

Both. 0 and 7 both mean Sunday in standard cron.

Why does my cron run on more days than expected?

If both day-of-month and day-of-week are set, cron runs when either matches. Set one of them to * to limit it.
